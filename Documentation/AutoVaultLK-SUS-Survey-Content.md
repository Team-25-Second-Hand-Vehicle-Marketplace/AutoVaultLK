## Scoring reference (for the evaluator, not shown on the form)

Standard SUS scoring on the 10 core items (Section 2):
- For odd-numbered items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): score = (response − 1)
- For even-numbered items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): score = (5 − response)
- Sum all 10 scores, multiply by 2.5 → SUS score out of 100.

An average score above ~68 is considered above-average usability; this is a
guide for interpretation, not a pass/fail threshold.
