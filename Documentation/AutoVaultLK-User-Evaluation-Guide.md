# AutoVaultLK — User Evaluation Guide and SUS Task Sheet

## 1. What AutoVaultLK Is

AutoVaultLK is a second-hand vehicle marketplace for Sri Lanka. Buyers search
and save listings; dealers list vehicles one at a time or upload a whole
inventory spreadsheet at once; admins verify dealers and monitor the
platform.

The walkthrough uses the web app. **Search** and the vehicle **detail page**
are the main buyer areas. The **Dealer portal** and **Admin portal** are
separate sections reached from their own login pages.

The purpose of this session is to evaluate how easy the system is to
understand and use. It is not a test of your technical knowledge. Please
describe anything that feels confusing, slow, unnecessary, or difficult.

## 3. The Walkthrough

Work through the tasks below in order, for whichever role(s) you were given.
If a task's account or feature was not provided to you, mark it **Not
available** and move to the next one.

### Part A — Buyer

1. **[Browser] Sign in as a buyer:** Open the system link and log in (or
   register a new account if you were not given one). Note whether it is
   clear where to begin.
2. **[Browser] Browse the homepage:** Identify the main navigation, the
   featured vehicles, and the vehicle category shortcuts (Cars, SUVs, Bikes,
   etc.).
3. **[Browser] Search by filters:** Go to the search page. Set at least two
   filters (for example, a vehicle type and a price range) and apply them.
   Confirm the results update and that the applied filters are shown as
   removable chips.
4. **[Browser] Search by typing a sentence:** In the search box, type a
   plain-language request such as *"toyota corolla under 8 million petrol"*
   (a small typo is fine — try misspelling the make on purpose, e.g.
   *"toyata"*). Confirm relevant results still appear.
5. **[Browser] Open a listing:** Click into a vehicle's detail page. Identify
   the price, specs, dealer information, and photos (or the placeholder
   photo, if none was uploaded).
6. **[Browser] Save a listing:** Use the save/heart button on a listing.
   Go to your Saved page and confirm it appears there.
7. **[Browser] Remove a saved listing:** Un-save it from either the card or
   the Saved page, and confirm it disappears.
8. **[Browser] Test an empty search:** Set filters unlikely to match anything
   (for example, a very low maximum price with a luxury make selected).
   Confirm the system explains what happened, rather than showing a blank
   page.
9. **[Browser] Sign out.**

### Part B — Dealer (if a dealer account was provided)

10. **[Browser] Sign in at the dealer login page** (a different login page
    from the buyer one). Note whether it is clear this is a separate area.
11. **[Browser] Review the dealer dashboard:** Identify your listing counts
    by status (Live, Pending review, Draft, Sold, etc.).
12. **[Browser] Create a listing:** Use the "add listing" form to enter one
    vehicle by hand. Note whether the required fields and their limits (year,
    price, etc.) are clear.
13. **[Browser] Edit a listing:** Open one of your existing listings and
    change a field (for example, the price), then save.
14. **[Browser] Bulk upload (business dealer accounts only):** Download the
    CSV template from the upload page. Using the **sample file supplied by
    the evaluator**, upload it and watch the status page update.
15. **[Browser] Review the upload result:** Once the upload finishes, check
    whether it says Completed or "Completed with skipped rows." If any rows
    were skipped, open the rejection report and note whether the reasons
    given are understandable.
16. **[Browser] Review your business profile:** Open the profile/business
    details page and note whether your verification status is clearly shown.
17. **[Browser] Sign out.**

### Part C — Admin (if an admin account was provided)

18. **[Browser] Sign in at the admin login page.**
19. **[Browser] Review the dashboard:** Identify the summary numbers shown.
20. **[Browser] Review users:** Open the Users section and locate a specific
    dealer or buyer account.
21. **[Browser] Review uploads:** Open the Uploads section, filter by status,
    and open one upload job's detail.
22. **[Browser] Review reports and audit logs:** Briefly check both sections
    exist and show data.
23. **[Browser] Sign out.**

The evaluator may ask you to think aloud, but should not explain where
controls are or complete tasks for you. The goal is to observe the normal
user experience.

## 4. Features Not Tested

This self-guided evaluation does not require uploading a real inventory
file, entering real payment or identity details, changing another account's
password, or performing destructive actions (deleting a listing or a user
account). These actions require administrator setup and are outside this
usability session.

Please judge only the features you actually used. Mention in the open
comment if any unavailable features made the system feel incomplete.

## 5. What the Evaluator Records

For each task, record **Completed**, **Partially completed**, or **Not
available**. Also record:
- Total time taken.
- Tasks where the participant needed help.
- Error messages or unexpected behavior.
- Places where the participant hesitated or became confused.
- Positive comments and suggested improvements.
